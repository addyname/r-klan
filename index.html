About Our Plight

On July 8, 2015, our minority family's lives were drastically and permanently changed by nine racist words. "Your family has no rights; planes have no rules." They were spoken proudly by the airport manager, Meadow Chase, who was responsible for the management of Ramona Airport.

Meadow Chase was already fully aware of the impact her words would have and had the backing of local public servants and co-conspirators. Unwittingly, we found ourselves in a vicious, hopeless, helpless landscape of fear and violence, unable to use our property for its intended purpose or live in peace, as we had done for the past fifteen years.
